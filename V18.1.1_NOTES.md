# TYSHI ZONE v18.1.1

- Rebuilt episode grid mode around episode thumbnails and one compact episode number. It prefers saved episode art, then matches public AniList legal-stream thumbnails by episode number, with title artwork as a fallback. The list mode remains the detailed title/status view, and franchise seasons stay in their own section.
- Made the episode tray scroll inside its own area on phones and desktop, with native touch panning and normal page scroll chaining at the tray edge.
- Corrected the watch-page search wrapper so it collapses to the left edge beside the logo and expands smoothly back across the header.
- Kept Top Airing banner artwork visible at phone widths and added a restrained full-row lift and glow on desktop hover.
- Moved the site footer editor into the Announcements admin section, beside the dated announcement controls, so it no longer takes space on the Overview page.
- Preserved the existing iframe sandbox and ad-shield behavior.

AniList may only list some legal streaming episodes for a title, so automatic thumbnail coverage depends on the public episode data available for that anime.
