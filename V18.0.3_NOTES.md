# TYSHI ZONE v18.0.3

## Anime card interactions
- Hovering an anime card lifts and scales the full card gently, keeping its title and artwork together. The hover outline brightens slightly and the plus button gets a clearer white hover state.
- The plus button opens watchlist choices: Plan to watch, Watching, or Completed. Existing watchlist storage is reused, and saved titles can be removed from the menu.
- Home anime rows have a little vertical breathing room so the card lift is not clipped by the horizontal scroller.

## Season tiles
- Season tile corners are square and their white borders are removed.
- Non-selected artwork is darker and more blurred; selected artwork remains clear and prominent.
