# TYSHI ZONE v19.12

## Changes

- Reordered desktop home sections while retaining the phone section order; expanded mobile poster rails to the screen edges and kept carousel drag behavior intact.
- Used saved anime titles and poster overrides consistently; made bookmark menus require a deliberate tap instead of triggering from a scroll gesture.
- Limited Related to direct side-story, spin-off, adaptation, and alternative entries; recommendation titles remain in You Might Also Like.
- Updated show card hover/reveal motion, season selection borders, theme accents, and compact Settings grouping.
- Changed Dub releases to use an explicit dub release date on each episode. The feed excludes episodes without a date and never treats an upload/save time or Japanese air date as a dub premiere.
- Removed the duplicate episode-list search from the watch page. The single header search submits to `/search?q=...`; on phones on the watch page it rests as a magnifier and smoothly expands left when focused. The search surface is lighter and more restrained.
- Made episode lists part of normal document flow, so scrolling can continue below the last episode on desktop and phones.
- Kept the compact About Anime redesign on the phone player page, removed the misleading unavailable-server line, and made phone player corners square in normal and fullscreen modes.
- Refreshes AniWeeb playback sessions without changing saved links; other provider URLs are reloaded unchanged by the refresh action.

## Dub release-date setup

In Admin → Episodes, set **Dub release date** on dubbed episodes. Only dated releases from the last 14 days appear in the Dub feed. Existing episode records have no dub premiere date, so they will not appear until an admin supplies one.
