# TYSHI ZONE v19.6

## Fullscreen right-edge gap (found)
The page keeps a permanent scrollbar (`overflow-y: scroll` plus `scrollbar-gutter: stable`). While a video was fullscreen that scrollbar space stayed reserved, so the video stopped about 15px short of the right edge (the same width as a scrollbar). Now, while anything is fullscreen, the page scrollbar and its gutter are switched off (`html.is-fullscreen` and `html:has(:fullscreen)`). Provider fullscreen and the iframe ad-shield sandbox are unchanged.

## Phone scrolling
- Up/down scrolling over New Releases, Continue Watching, You might also like, Related and the other rows was blocked by `touch-pan-x`. The rows now allow both horizontal and vertical panning.
- New Releases could not scroll sideways because its home-page wrapper widened the grid; the wrapper now has `min-w-0`.
- Video player: thin strips (phone only) on the left and right edges, above the control bar, let a finger scroll the page when it starts there. Taps on the video and its controls are unchanged.

## Fullscreen on phones
When the video goes fullscreen on a phone, the page asks the browser to lock the screen to landscape, and unlocks it when you exit. This works in browsers that allow it (Chrome/Brave on Android). iPhone Safari does not allow orientation locking, so it cannot be forced there.

## Episode list
- List view now scrolls inside the panel with a scrollbar in the theme colour; the number grid has no scrollbar.
- Episode pictures: Bulk import now gives every TMDB-mapped episode its own TMDB picture and episode name when it imports or updates a title (only where the episode has none yet, so custom pictures are kept). Titles imported earlier get theirs the next time you run Bulk import for them. Titles with no TMDB match still show AniList's pictures where AniList has them, otherwise the series artwork.

## Polish
Theme-coloured scrollbar class, crisper text rendering, and a short colour-only transition on all buttons and links (no movement).

Validation: production build and TypeScript check passed; ESLint formatting applied to the changed files. Not tried in a browser or phone.
