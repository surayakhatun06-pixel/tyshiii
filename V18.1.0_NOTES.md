# TYSHI ZONE v18.1.0

- Switched horizontal carousels back to native phone touch scrolling and momentum; mouse drag remains direct and click-safe.
- Removed mobile height caps from episode and related anime lists so phone swipes continue through the page instead of getting caught in nested scroll areas. Desktop episode and related panels retain independent scrolling.
- Reworked episode tiles to show `EP 01` and `Episode 1`, with a slim theme-colored marker on the selected episode and a compact three-column phone grid.
- Kept the app canvas and surfaces black across themes. Replaced large theme swatches with five compact choices: Black, Deep Violet, Ember Orange, Rose and Deep Teal.
- Added the animated notification bell and live, dated announcements. The admin panel can publish, edit and remove up to 100 announcements; visitor access is read-only through the existing `siteConfig` Firestore rules.
- Made direct video files crop to fill the player viewport when the source aspect ratio differs. The iframe sandbox and ad-shield behavior are unchanged.
- Kept earlier v18.0.8 work: Trending and Top Airing remain, Popular This Season stays removed, the footer remains admin-editable, and the server/language selection fixes remain in place.
