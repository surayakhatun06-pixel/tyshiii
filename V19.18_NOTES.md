# TYSHI ZONE v19.18 (built on 19.17)

## Batch 1  (DONE)

**Admin tabs and organization**
- The tab row scrolls by mouse drag, mouse wheel, touch and the new left/right arrow buttons (they appear only when tabs are hidden on that side). A drag never clicks a tab. The open tab scrolls into view by itself.
- Tabs are now: Overview, Universal Servers, Bulk import, Spotlight, Announcements, Extra options.
- **Extra options** holds Add episodes, My anime, Add anime or series and the older Servers tool.
- The Overview page is grouped: Links, Import, Site, and a collapsible "Extra options" section (remembered on this device).

**Watch page**
- The three-dots menu says **Reload player** (was "Refresh link & reload player") and **Auto-next** (also in Settings > Player).
- The search bar no longer changes size while the watch page loads. It collapses to the small icon only when YOU scroll the page.

**Seasons, parts and movies**
- New title reader (`src/lib/season-titles.ts`): understands Season N, 2nd Season, Second Season, Season Two, Part N, Cour N, 2nd Part, Final Season, and bare numbers / numerals (Mob Psycho 100 II, Overlord IV).
- A part with no season ("SAKAMOTO DAYS Part 2") stays inside its season: "Season 1 Part 1", "Season 1 Part 2". Two parts can no longer both become "Season 2".
- A TV part or season that AniList files as a side story / alternative / parent, but whose title shows it is part of the same series, now appears in **Seasons**, not under Related. The franchise walk also follows these links.
- Details page and watch page use the same builder, so they always agree.
- A title AniList marks as a movie counts as ONE movie (never "3 episodes") and shows "Movie". Movie cards no longer show "N ep".
